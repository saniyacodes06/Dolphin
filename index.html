Masters of the Sea

Dolphins are highly intelligent marine mammals and are part of the family of toothed whales that includes orcas and pilot whales. They are found worldwide, mostly in shallow seas of the continental shelves.

Known for their agility and playful behavior, they are deeply social creatures that live in pods, which can number up to a thousand individuals.

Fun Fact

Dolphins have names for each other. They use a unique whistle to identify themselves and can call out to specific individuals in their pod.

Species Showcase

Bottlenose Dolphin

Region: Worldwide

Lifespan: 40-50 years

The most well-known species, highly intelligent and adaptable.

Orca (Killer Whale)

Region: All Oceans

Lifespan: 50-90 years

The largest member of the dolphin family, apex predators of the sea.

Spinner Dolphin

Region: Tropical oceans

Lifespan: 20-25 years

Famous for their acrobatic displays and spinning leaps.

Common Dolphin

Region: Temperate and tropical waters

Lifespan: 35-40 years

Highly social and can form mega-pods of thousands of individuals.

Remarkable Intelligence

📡

Echolocation

Dolphins use biological sonar to navigate and hunt, producing high-frequency clicks that bounce off objects.

🧠

Complex Brain

Their brains are large and highly folded, with an Encephalization Quotient second only to modern humans.

💬

Communication

They possess a complex language of clicks, whistles, and body language to coordinate group activities.

🧩

Problem Solving

Known to use tools, such as sponges to protect their snouts while foraging on the sea floor.
